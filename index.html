Orceal Lab

Research on learning theory, algorithms, and reasoning in AI.

About

Orceal Lab is a research group focused on understanding the foundations of intelligent systems. We are motivated less by chasing benchmark performance and more by a deeper question: why do these systems learn what they learn, and what governs the limits of that learning? Our work spans theory and practice, aiming to build a rigorous understanding of learning, computation, and reasoning that can inform the next generation of AI systems.

Research Areas

Learning Theory

We study the mathematical foundations of learning: generalization, sample complexity, and the trade-offs that govern how models learn from data. Our goal is to explain, not just observe, why learning algorithms succeed or fail.

Algorithms

We design and analyze algorithms for optimization, inference, and decision-making under uncertainty. This work emphasizes provable guarantees alongside practical efficiency, connecting theory to real systems.

Reasoning in AI

We investigate how AI systems represent, manipulate, and reason over structured knowledge. This includes studying the emergence of reasoning capabilities in large models and how to make that reasoning more reliable and interpretable.
